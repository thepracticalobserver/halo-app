# Halo privacy

Halo collects **no data**. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What Halo uses on your phone

| Access | Why | Where it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display over other apps | To open the clock by itself when you plug in and turn the phone sideways | Nothing is read or stored |
| Motion and light sensors | To tell when the phone is sideways and still, and when the room is dark (Auto night) | Used live, never saved or shared |
| Calendar (read-only) | **Only if you pick the Agenda style.** Shows today's timed events on the clock | Read on the phone each time, never saved or shared. Events can show on the lock screen, so anyone who sees the phone sees the titles |
| Foreground service, wake lock, start at boot | To keep watching for charging and orientation, and to restart after a reboot | Nothing is stored |

Halo saves only your own choices (style, colour theme and a few switches) in the app's private storage on the phone. Uninstalling Halo deletes them.
